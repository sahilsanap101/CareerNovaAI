# Research Hypotheses

This section formalizes the testable statements corresponding to the Research Questions outlined previously.

**H1:**
Market-aware career recommendation improves ranking quality over the existing BYSER baseline.

**H2:**
Gap × career importance × market demand improves market-oriented skill coverage over gap-only prioritization.

**H3:**
Prerequisite constraints reduce invalid skill sequences without materially reducing useful market alignment.

**H4:**
Closed-loop re-planning decreases stale-plan rate after state changes while controlling roadmap churn.
