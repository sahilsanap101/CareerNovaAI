# Algorithmic Fairness Audit

Machine-learning-guided career mapping fundamentally runs severe risks of echoing structural societal biases mapped from generalized labor frequency pools. PathForge enforces rigid constraints avoiding bias generation through algorithmic isolation.

## Protected Attribute Expulsion
Mathematical matrices evaluating Career Alignment and Priority metrics explicitly forbid the integration of the following classes:
*   Name
*   Gender (unless experimentally justified and cleared via external IRB specific mapping parameters)
*   Religion
*   Caste
*   Ethnicity
*   Political Affiliation
*   Financial Status
*   Health Information

## The Input Audit
The engine validates calculations natively off **Skill Vectors** alone (`Gap`, `Time Allocation Budget`, `Priorities`). By isolating evaluation to technical gap-bridging capabilities bounded by external market demand, the algorithm treats all identical node inputs equally across identically bounded students safely detached from generalized socioeconomic constraints.
