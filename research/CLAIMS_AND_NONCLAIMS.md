# Claims and Non-Claims

To adhere to rigorous academic standards and avoid hyperbolic assertions common in commercial EdTech software, this document explicitly defines the boundaries of the framework's effectiveness.

## Explicit Non-Claims (What we do NOT assert)
- The system does **NOT** guarantee employment or job placement.
- The system does **NOT** produce the absolute "best" or universally optimal roadmap for all humans in all contexts.
- The system does **NOT** operate in literal "real-time" continuous feedback; re-planning occurs at discrete, measurable state-change intervals.
- The system does **NOT** claim to be "highly accurate" in predicting human career trajectories across decades.
- The system does **NOT** claim generalized AGI-level "intelligent" reasoning.
- The system does **NOT** output deterministic "98% confidence" bounds unless directly derived from closed-form probabilistic calculations that are experimentally supported by the dataset.

## Validated Claims (Restricted to Empirical Support)
All claims made in the ensuing IEEE publication will be strictly bound to:
1. Improvements mathematically measured and tested against defined baselines (e.g., "Demonstrates a statistically significant increase in market-oriented coverage relative to the baseline").
2. Outcomes observed within the designated experimental dataset boundaries.
3. Formally bounded optimizations (e.g., "Maximizes utility function $U$ within defined graph constraint $P$").
