# Human Oversight & Transparency Logs

A foundational risk of adaptive logic models is algorithmic "black-boxing" yielding un-reproducible shifts in student requirements.

## 1. Transparency Output Matrix
The framework prevents opaque output states by natively exporting granular explanatory data with every single Roadmap iteration tracking:
- **Evidence Used**: What assessment metrics justified the $Gap()$ calculation.
- **Skills Possessed vs Missing**: Explicit delta enumerations.
- **Market Information Utilized**: Exactly what job alignment vectors fed the $Priority()$ logic.
- **Prerequisites Resolved**: Structural Topological bounds explicitly explaining phase-locks (*e.g., "Deferred Python ML due to unresolved calculus blocks"*).

## 2. Infrastructure Logging
Each simulated evaluation generates a strict `Auditor-Log` maintaining metadata states for identical reconstruction pipelines capturing:
1. `Recommendation_Explanation_ID`
2. `Data_Provenance_Matrix` (What explicit O*NET/Synthetic snapshot drove the engine)
3. `Algorithm_Version` (Baseline B2 vs Tracker B4)
4. `Model_Version` (Specific parametric constants)
5. `Roadmap_Version` ($R_t$)
