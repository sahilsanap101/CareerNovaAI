# B1 Market-Aware Career Ranker Formulation

The B1 model introduces dynamic external labor market constraints into the internal student optimization graph.

## The Model
$$ CareerScore(u,c,t) = \alpha \cdot SkillFit(u,c) + \beta \cdot GoalFit(u,c) + \gamma \cdot InterestFit(u,c) + \delta \cdot MarketAlignment(u,c,t) $$

### Base Normalization
All inputs to the scoring model must be monotonically bounded within $[0, 1]$ before scaling.
$$ \forall x \in \{SkillFit, GoalFit, InterestFit, MarketAlignment\}: x \in [0, 1] $$
$$ \alpha + \beta + \gamma + \delta = 1.0 $$

### 1. SkillFit ($u, c$)
Calculates the magnitude of career $c$'s skill requirements currently met by student $u$, penalized for missing prerequisite thresholds in the Formal Graph.
$$ SkillFit = \frac{\sum (ReqWeight_i \times \min(1.0, Proficiency_i))}{\sum ReqWeight_i} $$

### 2. GoalFit ($u, c$)
A binary or partial boolean logic representing intent matching (Target Role, Target Industry).
$$ GoalFit = (c.role == u.role) ? 1.0 : (c.industry == u.industry) ? 0.5 : 0.0 $$

### 3. InterestFit ($u, c$)
Semantic intersection between the domains the career $c$ touches and the stated interests of user $u$.
$$ InterestFit = \frac{| c.tags \cap u.interests |}{| c.tags |} (\text{bounded max 1.0}) $$

### 4. MarketAlignment ($u, c, t$)
A dynamic modifier relying on the `JobMarketSkillDemandEngine`. Asserts that a career with high localized demand increases total utility compared to a saturated market.
$$ MarketAlignment(u,c,t) = \frac{\sum_{s \in (c.skills \cap u.missingSkills)} CareerDemand(s,c,t)}{|c.skills|} $$

*(Note: Prioritizes pathways where the requisite skills are highly demanded currently.)*

## Parameter Sensitivity Range
- $\alpha \in [0.2, 0.6]$: Heavy weighting strictly favors immediate employment over reskilling.
- $\delta \in [0.1, 0.5]$: Defines how aggressively the system steers away from decaying markets.

## Interpretability
Score explanations are deterministically generated by observing the highest contributing deltas. Modalities combine string templates addressing:
1. "Skills already possessed"
2. "Important missing skills that are in high-demand"
3. "Goal alignment hits"
