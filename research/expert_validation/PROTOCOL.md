# Expert Evaluation Protocol

To circumvent internal validation bias impacting algorithm evaluation claims, this double-blind protocol is established. 

## Eligibility Criteria
Evaluators recruited MUST verify formal compliance with at least one target demographic constraint without their Personally Identifiable Information (PII) mapping into the final dataset:
- Minimum $5+$ years academic instruction focused on computer engineering/technical curriculum (Faculty).
- Active Technical Recruiters evaluating skills-based resumes (Industry).
- Senior-level Engineers acting autonomously as technical domain interviewers.

## Anonymization and Blinding
- The generated experimental JSONs (spanning outcomes generated by legacy B0 vs Proposed A6 roadmaps) are universally stripped of algorithm tags. Evaluators simply score "Path A vs Path B" unaware of which is numerically unconstrained or heuristically bucketed.
- Demographic identities are exclusively masked as cryptographically secure generic GUIDs (e.g., `expert_d8a391`). Names, contact arrays, and IPs are forcefully blocked from all database architectures.

## Execution Constraints
Experts are sequentially presented:
1. An explicit Student Initial-State Matrix ($S_{initial}$).
2. An isolated Target Goal ($C_{target}$).
3. The generated roadmap timeline sequence.
They proceed iteratively traversing the `RUBRIC.md` limits until dataset evaluation completion.
