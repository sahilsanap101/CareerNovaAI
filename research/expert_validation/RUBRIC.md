# Expert Evaluation Rubric

Human oversight provides qualitative grounding confirming that mathematical DAG traversal and prioritization algorithms do not violate nuanced contextual industry intelligence. Evaluators rank outputs via a strictly mapped 5-point Likert scale (1 = `Strongly Disagree`, 5 = `Strongly Agree`).

## The 8 Grading Criteria

1. **Career Relevance**: "The statistically-recommended roles strictly align with the student's initial proficiency distributions and stated categorical interests."
2. **Skill-Gap Correctness**: "The explicit deficits identified natively bridge the true requirements between current proficiency and target industry standardization."
3. **Skill-Priority Correctness**: "The mathematically extracted order prioritizes foundational macro-concepts alongside empirically high-value market capabilities properly."
4. **Market-Alignment Plausibility**: "The justification reflecting global market demand appropriately correlates to observed real-world labor trends."
5. **Prerequisite Correctness**: "The internal topological sorting properly restricts advanced dependencies structurally preventing out-of-order learning."
6. **Roadmap Sequence Correctness**: "The chronologic traversal generation is cleanly phased without excessive or irrational cognitive shifting."
7. **Roadmap Actionability**: "The resulting temporal phases obey temporal constraints and feel highly achievable for standard students."
8. **Explanation Quality**: "The deterministically appended justifications ('Blocked by X', etc.) are logically coherent, clear, and accurately articulate the algorithmic boundary condition."
