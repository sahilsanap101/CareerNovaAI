# Expert Analysis Mathematics

The validation framework guarantees qualitative subjectivity remains rigorously bounded by standardized statistical matrices calculating significance parameters.

## Baseline Aggregations
All raw datasets map directly into:
1. **Mean ($\mu$)**: Tracks overall empirical superiority spanning identical models tested.
2. **Standard Deviation ($SD$)**: Calculates rating variance width highlighting volatile confidence models.

## Reliability Validation
To prove validation wasn't hijacked by disparate evaluator extremes natively sabotaging the outputs, testing integrates formal metric convergence.
- **Inter-Class Correlation (ICC) or Fleiss' Kappa**: Ensures evaluator convergence (Inter-Rater Agreement). If scoring matrices yield wild variances measuring algorithmic outcomes, the underlying rubric or algorithms are empirically suspect. A correlation bounding coefficient $> 0.75$ indicates excellent subjective agreement spanning independent blinded testers.

## Output Synthesizers
The `expert_analysis_runner` computationally maps CSV inputs isolating Model A paths vs Model B spanning 8 subjective dimensions resulting mathematically in explicit $p$-value tracking matrices assessing objective qualitative shifts.
