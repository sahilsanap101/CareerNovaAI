# Research Questions

The implementation and evaluation of the framework are structured to formally answer the following specific research questions:

**RQ1:**
Does incorporating job-market skill demand improve career ranking compared with static profile-based recommendation?

**RQ2:**
Does combining student skill gap, career importance, and market demand improve skill-priority selection compared with gap-only or market-only approaches?

**RQ3:**
Does prerequisite-constrained planning improve roadmap feasibility while retaining career-market utility?

**RQ4:**
Does closed-loop re-planning improve plan relevance after changes to the student skill state or market state while limiting unnecessary roadmap changes?
