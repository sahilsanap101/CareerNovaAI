# Responsible AI Declarations

## Core Disclaimer Matrix
Any interaction exposing PathForge outputs to physical testers requires the explicitly injected rendering of the following computational bound statement:

> **"PathForge provides decision support and does not determine a student's career outcome."**

## System Limitations
PathForge algorithms are mathematical optimizers constrained strictly by inputs. They compute statistical probabilities, not undeniable destiny.
- **Assumptions**: The system mathematically assumes that specific sub-skills accurately map total job viability. It assumes job market indicators remain stable through the temporal gap. 
- **Uncertainty**: Priority scores are estimates. If `React` is assigned a Market Alignment of $0.85$, that metric bounds a historic variance distribution. The algorithms do not possess deterministic foresight.

To prevent algorithm-dependence syndrome, no generated career recommendation ranking shall ever be presented technically as a "guaranteed" or "authoritative" directive spanning a user's life.
